# Explaining results

- **Clean pass:** "The device passed N of N checks." Do not say "secure." Explain these checks can't see side-channel, fault injection, relay, social engineering, or a perfect-descriptor implant. Suggest a second source: the vendor's genuine-device check in the official app, and cross-checking receive addresses with another wallet (e.g. HWI) before moving funds.
- **Pass with conditions:** read each warning and its next steps. Common steps: fix permissions and rerun; use the vendor genuine check; buy direct from the vendor; move funds to a newly set-up device whose seed was generated on the device.
- **Red light:** say plainly what was caught. Tell the user not to use the device for funds, not to enter any seed on it, and to contact the vendor through its official site. If funds are on it, help plan moving them using a separate trusted device.
